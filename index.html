    README.md

    Curious first, polished later.

    I learn by building: opening an existing project, changing one thing, and seeing what breaks. A lot of my best understanding comes from a stack trace I didn't expect.

    Right now that means a Python agent that works through tasks using tools, this site, and a few dark, minimal interfaces I keep rebuilding until they feel right.

    I'm still developing as an engineer, and I'd rather show an honest, unfinished experiment than a perfect-looking claim. Anything on this site that is early, rough or local-only is labelled that way.
